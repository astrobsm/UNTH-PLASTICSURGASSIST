📝 How to Book Your Appointment — Step by Step

1

Scan the QR Code

Open your phone's camera app and point it at the QR code above. Tap the link that appears on your screen. Alternatively, you can type the web address shown below the QR code into any browser.

2

Select a Clinic Date

Choose from the available clinic dates displayed on the page. Clinic days are Tuesdays (three doctors) and Wednesdays (two doctors). Only dates with open slots will be shown.

3

Pick a Time Slot

Select a convenient time slot from the available options. Green slots are open; grey slots are already booked. Tuesday slots are 25 minutes, Wednesday slots are 20 minutes.

4

Enter Your Details

Fill in your Full Name (required), Phone Number / WhatsApp (required), and Hospital Number (optional — if you have one from a previous visit). Then read and agree to the terms.

5

Confirm Your Booking

Tap "Book Appointment" to submit. You will see a confirmation screen with your assigned doctor, date, time, and arrival time (1 hour before appointment).

6

Share via WhatsApp

After booking, tap the green "Send Confirmation via WhatsApp" button to save your appointment details. Share it with family members so they can help you remember.

7

Arrive 1 Hour Early

On your appointment day, arrive at least 1 hour before your scheduled time to: complete vital signs, pay consultation fees, and confirm availability of wound care materials (if needed).

⚠️ Important Reminders

  • Arrive at least 1 hour before your appointment time.
  • Late patients will be seen only after all scheduled patients.
  • Missed appointments must be rescheduled through the booking system.
  • Bring your hospital card and any previous medical records.
  • In case of emergency, contact the unit directly — do not wait for your appointment.
