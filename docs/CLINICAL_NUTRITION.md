# Clinical nutrition module

This module covers nutrition assessment, requirement calculation, monitoring and meal planning for adult patients. It is clinical decision support. Every assessment and plan stays a draft until a dietitian, consultant or senior registrar approves it, and every output carries the notice *"Generated by decision-support software. Review before clinical use."*

## Where it appears

| Place | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Patient profile → Clinical Nutrition** (`?section=nutrition`) | The patient's nutrition record, in six tabs: Overview, Assessment, Measurements, Food chart, Meal plan, Trends. It also produces the PDFs. |
| **Clinical Nutrition** in the sidebar (`/nutrition`) | The dietitian worklist: highest risk first, with what needs doing (approval, reassessment, refeeding protocol). |
| `/nutrition/foods` | The food composition database: the WAFCT 2019 import, the Enugu local layer, the recipe builder, household measures, and validation and sign-off. |
| Ward round, burns, pre-op workup, pressure sore, limb salvage, sickle cell ulcer, post-op note, treatment plan, discharge | A `NutritionSummaryCard` showing the risk level, targets, refeeding risk, alerts and plan status, with a link to the record. |
| Discharge summary | **Use nutrition meal plan** takes the patient's approved plan from the record. A draft plan is labelled as a draft. |
| Registration, bedside calculators | A provisional plan from the same engine and solver, solved against the stated targets. |

There is only one nutrition engine (`src/clinical/nutrition`). Nothing else in the app calculates nutrition requirements or builds meal plans. The old generators, including the hand-typed `nigerianFoods.ts` values, were removed in stage 6.

## Architecture

```
src/clinical/nutrition/
  config/nutrition.v1.ts  every coefficient and threshold, with its source (versioned)
  anthropometry.ts        knee height / ulna stature; knee height + MUAC weight; MUAC BMI band;
                          amputation; IBW (Devine); adjusted body weight; % change; MAMC; grip
  screening.ts            MUST, NRS-2002, SGA, GLIM, (m)NUTRIC
  requirements.ts         energy equations, protein, weight goal, refeeding, fluid,
                          micronutrients, nitrogen balance, scope gate
  assess.ts               assessNutrition(inputs) → complete traceable assessment
  monitoring.ts           schedule, six alerts, intake conversion, recalculation diff
  foods.ts                nutrient arithmetic from per-100 g table values; recipes
  planner.ts              weekly menu + linear programme (javascript-lp-solver 0.4.24, pinned)
  record.ts               patient record → inputs, alerts, due checks, planner options
  formats.ts / pdf.ts     discharge rendering; clinician / dietitian / patient PDFs
  checklist.ts            validation checklist generated from the active config
api/nutrition.js + api/_lib/nutrition.js    storage, roles, approvals, audit
api/_lib/data/wafct2019.js                  GENERATED from the FAO workbook
api/_lib/data/enuguFoods.js                 Enugu layer (draft recipes, portions, Igbo names)
api/_lib/data/nutritionKinds.js             measurement kinds, units and ranges (API and forms)
database/migrations/024_clinical_nutrition.*.sql
```

**Traceability.** Every result has the shape `{ value, method, inputs, assumptions, source }`. Every assessment stores `config_version`. A meal plan also stores `food_data_version`.

**Where values live.** Weights recorded on the vitals chart and analytes from completed lab orders are read, never copied. The module stores only what it measures itself, in `nutrition_measurements`.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Dietitian (new built-in role), consultant, senior registrar | Approve assessments and meal plans; sign off coefficient sets |
| Registrar, house officer, medical officer | Draft and submit assessments and plans |
| Nurse (`nurse` / `nursing`) | Record measurements and the food chart only |
| Lab, pharmacy | No access |
| Dietitian, consultant, admin | Maintain food data, recipes and household measures |

Every write goes to `clinical_audit_events` through `auditClinical`. Records are corrected by marking them entered-in-error with a reason; nothing is deleted. Nutrition data is health data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. It uses the same authentication, audit and access controls as the rest of the clinical record. External AI is not used.

## Data sources

### WAFCT 2019 (primary)

- **Source.** The FAO/INFOODS Food Composition Table for Western Africa (2019): <https://www.fao.org/fileadmin/user_upload/faoweb/2020/WAFCT_2019.xlsx>
- **Required citation.** Vincent A., Grande F., Compaoré E., et al. 2020. *FAO/INFOODS Food Composition Table for Western Africa (2019) User Guide & Condensed Food Composition Table.* Rome, FAO.
- **Licence.** © FAO 2020. Use is permitted in non-commercial products or services, with acknowledgement of FAO as source and copyright holder. Commercial use needs FAO's permission (copyright@fao.org).
- **Coverage.** 1,028 foods. Values are per 100 g edible portion: energy, protein, fat, carbohydrate, fibre, water, Na, K, Ca, Fe, Zn, P, Mg, vitamin A (RAE), vitamin C and folate (DFE). The import also keeps the edible-portion coefficient, the yield factor, and FAO's imputed flags (values FAO prints in [brackets]).

To update the table:

```
node scripts/nutrition/import-wafct.mjs path/to/WAFCT_2019.xlsx   # regenerates api/_lib/data/wafct2019.js
```

Then go to **Food database → Source & import → Re-import WAFCT 2019**. Re-importing updates nutrient values only. Local names, portions, recipes and measures your team has edited are kept.

### Nigerian Food Composition Table (secondary)

Not included, because we have no licensed copy. To add it, generate rows in the same shape and post them to `POST /api/nutrition?action=import_foods` with a new `source.code`.

### Enugu layer

`api/_lib/data/enuguFoods.js` sits on top of the WAFCT codes and adds:
- local foods and their Igbo names;
- planner portion limits and unit sizes (one egg, one piece of meat);
- cost tier, availability and season;
- 23 draft standardised recipes, built only from WAFCT ingredients;
- draft household measures.

Applying it (**Apply Enugu layer**) inserts recipes with status *Draft* and measures as *unverified*. It never overwrites a recipe or measure your team has edited.

### Local foods not in WAFCT 2019

Fluted pumpkin (ugu), oha, ugba, stockfish, kpomo (cow skin), semolina, and the soup spices (uziza, utazi, ehuru) have no WAFCT entry. They can't be planned until a verified source is added. No substitute values are used; WAFCT's *Cucurbita* pumpkin leaves are not ugu.

## Changing coefficients

1. Copy `src/clinical/nutrition/config/nutrition.v1.ts` to `nutrition.v2.ts`. Change the values and sources, and set `version: '2.0.0'` and `status: 'pending_clinical_validation'`.
2. Point `config/index.ts` at the new file.
3. Run `npx vitest run src/test/nutrition*`. The worked examples in `nutritionEngine.test.ts` are calculated by hand from the coefficients, so a changed coefficient shows up there as a deliberate test change.
4. After deployment, an approver signs off the new version under **Food database → Validation & sign-off**.

Never edit a version that has produced approved plans, because those plans cite it. Assessments made under an older version show "recalculate" on the Overview tab.

## Validation before go-live

**Food database → Validation & sign-off** lists:
- every coefficient and threshold, generated live from the active config;
- every draft recipe;
- every unverified household measure;
- the local foods that have no source values.

It exports a printable checklist PDF and records the sign-off. Items the clinical team should look at most closely:

- **Population equations.** The Chumlea knee-height and weight equations, and the BAPEN ulna table. The ulna table comes from a UK population, and no Nigerian equation was found.
- **Grip strength cut-offs.** The EWGSOP2 cut-offs are UK-derived; no Nigerian reference values were found.
- **UNTH defaults chosen here:**
  - "prealbumin falling" means a drop of 20% or more;
  - weight changing faster than planned means more than 1.5 times the planned rate over 2 weeks or more;
  - protein targets within each band;
  - a large wound means more than 25 cm²;
  - a burn counts as catabolic up to 60 days after the injury;
  - the default energy split is 50/20/30 (carbohydrate, protein, fat), with carbohydrate at 45% or less in diabetes;
  - fever adds about 10% fluid per °C;
  - the monitoring schedule by risk level;
  - each dish may be served at 0.5–2× its standard portion (swallows 1.6× at most).
- **Recipes and measures.** Every recipe weight and household measure has to be checked by local weighing.

## Out of scope (v1)

Patients under 18, pregnancy and lactation, dialysis-dependent renal failure, and parenteral nutrition are detected. For these patients the module still screens, but shows *"Specialist dietitian/physician input required"* and produces no targets or meal plan.
